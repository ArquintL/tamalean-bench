Tamarin
-------
Run here, one lemma at a time, in the model's directory: tamarin-prover +RTS -N4 -M8G -RTS
--heuristic=O --oraclename=Contact.oracle --prove=<lemma> Contact.spthy
Where the flags come from: --heuristic=O --oraclename: the command the authors give.
