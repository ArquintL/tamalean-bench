Tamarin
-------
Run here, one lemma at a time, in the model's directory: tamarin-prover +RTS -N4 -M8G -RTS
--derivcheck-timeout=0 --prove=<lemma> model.spthy
Where the flags come from: --derivcheck-timeout=0: the authors' command; the oracle is named in
the theory file (no flag needed).

Measured for this benchmark: not run yet.

Reported by the authors: prove-auto.sh: 8h, 10 GB; prove-expensive.sh: 8h, 100 GB (README;
elsewhere the README says the expensive lemma took around 1-2 days)
Hardware the authors state: Ubuntu 24.04, 252 GB, 2x Intel Xeon E5-2650 v4 (48 threads) (README)
Tamarin version the authors used: 1.8.0 with Maude 3.1 and 3.2.1 (README)

Lean side
---------
In scope (trace properties): Bench/pq3/model.lean reads the model with Tamalean's .spthy reader
and states one certify per lemma; scripts/run_lean.py records the outcome in
results/tamalean/pq3/model.json.
