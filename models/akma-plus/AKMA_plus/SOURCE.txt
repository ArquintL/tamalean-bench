Tamarin
-------
Run here, one lemma at a time, in the model's directory: tamarin-prover +RTS -N4 -M8G -RTS
--auto-sources --derivcheck-timeout=100 --quit-on-warning --prove=<lemma> AKMA_plus.spthy
Where the flags come from: --auto-sources: the authors' command (README or Makefile);
--derivcheck-timeout=100: the authors' command; --quit-on-warning: the authors' command; the
oracle is named in the theory file (no flag needed).

Measured for this benchmark: not run yet.

Hardware the authors state: 16 GB RAM minimum, 32 GB recommended (README)
Tamarin version the authors used: 1.8.0 (README)
